# Personas

**Instagram/WhatsApp seller (primary)** — runs a jewelry, fashion, beauty or home-goods business entirely
through DMs today. Not technical. Manages the business on a phone. Needs: a store link to put in their bio,
a way to accept payment without arguing over UPI screenshots, and simple order tracking. This is who the
onboarding wizard, mobile-first storefront and COD-first payment flow are built for.

**Home baker / small D2C brand** — slightly more established, may already take pre-orders via a spreadsheet
or WhatsApp broadcast list. Wants collections/categories, basic discounts for regulars, and to look more
"official" than a DM-based business — the polished storefront theme and store link matter for credibility
here.

**Reseller / boutique owner** — manages more SKUs (10s–100s of products), cares more about inventory accuracy
and order fulfillment speed than storefront aesthetics. This persona is the first to need bulk product status
changes, low-stock visibility and staff accounts (Business plan).

**Platform admin (internal)** — Sellly's own team, monitoring merchant health, platform revenue, abuse and
support load across all stores. Not merchant-facing; served by `apps/admin`.
