# Product Vision

**Turn your social audience into a store.**

Sellers on Instagram and WhatsApp already have an audience and a product — what they lack is the
infrastructure to sell without manually handling every DM, payment screenshot and shipping address. Sellly
converts that manual workflow into a real online store in minutes, without requiring any e-commerce or
technical knowledge.

**Core workflow:** Instagram / WhatsApp → Store Link → Product → Cart → Checkout → Payment → Order → Shipping
→ Notification.

**Product strategy:** don't position Sellly as "another Shopify." The initial product optimizes for the
narrowest, sharpest job — the fastest path from social audience to a published store to a first order. Broader
SaaS commerce capability (advanced marketing automation, deep shipping integrations, multi-channel) is real,
but it's earned after the core loop is validated, not built speculatively upfront. Long-term differentiation
comes from AI-assisted store creation and product enrichment, layered on top of a fast, reliable commerce
core — not from feature breadth.

**Activation goal:** a new merchant should be able to publish a basic store in under 10 minutes.
