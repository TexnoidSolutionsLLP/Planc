# Brand

**Working product name:** Sellly
**Positioning:** Turn your social audience into a store.

Sellly is not positioned as "another Shopify." It's the fastest way for someone already selling through DMs to
get a real store, without learning e-commerce vocabulary. The brand voice is direct, encouraging and never
condescending — most users are running a business on their phone between replying to customers.

The visual identity is quiet by design: near-black text on white, a single accent color (`#5B5BF7`) used
sparingly for interactive emphasis, and Inter as the only typeface. Nothing about the brand should compete
with the merchant's own product photography once their store is live — the platform chrome recedes.
